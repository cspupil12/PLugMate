Urban EV Challenge

Why Community Charger Sharing Matters

Modeled after Co Charger UK to bridge the urban infrastructure gap across Indian cities.

🚫

The "No-Driveway" Dilemma

Over 60% of urban EV owners in cities like Ludhiana live in multi-story apartments, builder floors, or rented townhomes. DISCOM rules and shared parking make installing personal 7.4 kW chargers nearly impossible.

  • ✕ Waiting hours at expensive public DC fast chargers
  • ✕ Battery degradation from excessive DC fast charging
  • ✕ Unsafe hanging extension cables across streets
🤝

The VoltShare Solution

Neighborhood homeowners with private driveways, solar arrays, or idle wallboxes list their chargers on VoltShare. Non-driveway neighbors book overnight slots, plug in, and walk home.

  • ✓ Safe, enclosed overnight driveway parking
  • ✓ Gentle 7.4 kW AC charging preserves EV battery health
  • ✓ Hosts earn 90% net payout on surplus solar power
