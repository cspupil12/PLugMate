Why Driveway Sharing Works

Apartment EV owners rarely have access to private sockets, while residential wallboxes sit idle most of the day.

The Apartment Charging Gap

Drivers living in multi-unit buildings or rented homes often rely on crowded public DC stations or unsafe extension cords.

  • - Long queues at highway fast chargers
  • - Higher battery degradation from constant DC fast charging
  • - No safe overnight parking near home

Peer-to-Peer Residential AC

Homeowners list idle wallboxes and solar ports with fixed hourly pricing, gated entry, and verified driver check-ins.

  • + Enclosed driveway parking within walking distance
  • + Gentle 3.3 kW to 22 kW AC charging speeds
  • + 90% host payout released after PIN verification

Charging Session Flow

01 / DISCOVER & BOOK

Pick a Charger & Time Window

Search by city or connector type on the map, inspect hourly rates, and reserve your slot with an instant booking reference.

02 / 4-DIGIT PIN HANDSHAKE

Verify Arrival at the Driveway

Follow turn-by-turn road directions to the host's gate and share your unique 4-digit PIN so the host can unlock power.

03 / PAYOUT & REVIEW

Escrow Release & Rating

Once the host confirms your PIN, escrow payout credits to the host dashboard and you can rate the charging visit.
