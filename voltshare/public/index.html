Why Driveway Sharing Works

Connecting electric vehicle owners with neighbors who have idle chargers and solar driveways.

The Multi-Unit Dilemma

Over 50% of urban EV drivers live in apartments, shared floors, or rented residences where installing a dedicated wallbox is prohibited or cost-prohibitive.

  • ✕ Waiting in queues at commercial DC fast chargers
  • ✕ Faster battery wear from continuous DC rapid-charging
  • ✕ Unsafe extension cables hanging out of upper-floor windows
🤝

The PlugMate Solution

Homeowners with private driveways, solar arrays, or idle wallboxes open their chargers to nearby neighbors. Drivers plug in, walk home, and return to a full battery.

  • ✓ Safe, enclosed overnight parking in neighbor driveways
  • ✓ Gentle AC charging that extends EV battery life
  • ✓ Hosts monetize surplus power and earn net payout
